# Design Tokens & Carbon Light Theme

Tiyatronot embraces an editorial, printed-playbill aesthetic derived from IBM Carbon Design System principles, styled specifically for the Turkish theatre context.

## Surface Hierarchy

| Token | Hex | Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `surface-canvas` | `#FFFFFF` | Viewport backdrop |
| `surface-layer-01` | `#F4F4F4` | Cards, input fields, subtle panels (Carbon Gray 10) |
| `surface-layer-02` | `#E0E0E0` | Elevated cards, hovered states, modal surfaces (Carbon Gray 20) |
| `surface-overlay` | `rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.45)` | Backdrop scrim |

## Typography Pairing

* **Headings & Display:** Classical Serifs (`Newsreader`, `Playfair Display`, `Georgia`) invoking printed stage playbills.
* **Body & UI:** Clean Sans-serif (`IBM Plex Sans`, `Inter`) for optimal screen readability.
* **Data & Timings:** Monospace (`IBM Plex Mono`) for curtain times, intermission splits, seat coordinates, and ticket tiers.

## Theatrical Accents

* `theatre-curtain` (`#BA1B23`): Crimson red indicating live shows, "now on stage" markers, and premier tags.
* `stage-spotlight` (`#F1C21B`): Warm gold reserved for ratings, laurels, and doyen badges.
* `interactive-primary` (`#0F62FE`): Carbon Blue 60 for primary actions and focused tabs.
